Status
Ongoing since 2022

Question addressed: How do we place and verify tower foundations safely on steep, weathered Mid-Himalayan slopes?

Methodology: Walked tower alignments; identified exposed rock and soil units; logged test pits at each footing; took dumpy-level height checks; screened cut-slopes for landslide and erosion risk before and during excavation.

My contribution: As Civil Sub-Engineer I supervised footing construction end-to-end — inspection sign-offs, quantity records, daily logs and hazard flags to the design team.

Deliverables: Verified foundations erected across the assigned stretch; continuous daily technical and quantity logs; documented hazard mitigations adopted on site.

Academic Record · Research

Research & Publications

Academic research kept separate from professional site work — each record lists the research question, methods, my role, status and findings.

Type
Academic research (undergraduate)
Location
Rasuwagadhi–Kerung transboundary corridor, Rasuwa District
Status
Published — peer-reviewed student journal

Research question: What drives mountain flood hazards along the transboundary Trishuli-headwater corridor, and how vulnerable are the settlements and the Kodari–Rasuwagadhi trade route?

Methodology: Field visit to the corridor; channel and geomorphological observation; review of local hazard records; disaster-risk analysis of terrain vulnerability.

My contribution: Co-authored the study — field data collection, hazard mapping input and manuscript preparation.

Findings / output: Identified channel-bottleneck and glacial-lake outburst contributions to flood peaks and recommended corridor-level risk-management measures. Full citation below.
